## 聊天气

It's rainy. I didn‘t take my umbrella.

**It seems that** we need to brave the rain. 看来我们好像得淋雨了哦。

Yeah, I don't want to be stuck here even if I'll get wet.

Come on. Let's go. Otherwise we're going to get soaked. 快点走吧，不然我们要被浇透了。


I'm stuck here in the pouring rain.  

You are dripping.  你身上在滴水哎。

- be stuck 被困住
- be stuck in the rain 被困在雨中
- brave the rain 淋雨（勇敢的；勇敢面对）
- get wet 打湿
- get soaked 被浇透；湿透的
- pouring rain 倾盆大雨 （pour 倾泻）
- baking hot 热得跟火炉一样 （bake 烤；烘焙）
- pass out 晕倒


What's the weather like outside?

## 俚语 Slang


- lit 亮眼；酷炫；  
    lit the light 点亮灯  
    Wow，this party is so lit.  
- sick 厉害的；酷炫的；  
    The party/show is sick.  

- savage 牛；厉害 / 野蛮的；残暴的    
    a savage world 一个野蛮的世界
    He‘s savage 他太牛了

- wack 怪人；怪胎；差劲的  
    It‘s really wack. 

- crap 废物；废话；非常差劲的；糟透的   
    The movie is crap. 电影是个烂片

- extra 额外的；多余的；戏多的  
    You‘re so extra.  你戏好多啊  
    You‘re a drama queen/king. 你是个戏精

- over the top 浮夸的；过分的  
    I think that was a little over the top.  
    I think this is a little over the top.  我觉得那有点过分哎。


- throw shade at/on someone 黒某人   
    We throw shade at politicians. 我们黑政府官员。    
    Don't throw shade on me, guys. 大家就别黑我了。

- suck it up.  控制情绪，忍一忍，算了
